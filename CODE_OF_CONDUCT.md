# Code of conduct

PiCache follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and every
other project space, whoever you talk to.

- Welcome: helpful and specific feedback, patience with newcomers, focus on
  the problem rather than the person, and accepting that others see things
  differently.
- Not acceptable: harassment, insults, personal or political attacks,
  sexualised language or imagery, publishing others' private information,
  and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues
and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban people
from the project temporarily or permanently.

**Reporting.** Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer,
[@Hustenreizjuengling](https://github.com/Hustenreizjuengling): open an issue
that only asks for a private contact (without details), and you will get a
private way to send the report. Reports are handled confidentially.
Security vulnerabilities are reported as described in
[docs/SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md) instead.
